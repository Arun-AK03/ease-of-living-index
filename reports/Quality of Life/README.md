# reports / Quality of Life

Generated charts for the **Quality of Life** sub-index, produced by
`notebooks/Quality of life_Visualisation.ipynb`.

## Figures
- `crimeindex.png` — crime / safety indicator.
- `imputed_distributions.png` — distributions of indicators after imputation
  (a sanity check that imputed values look reasonable).
- `quality_of_life_index.png` — the final quality-of-life index values.

Regenerate by re-running `notebooks/Quality of life_Visualisation.ipynb`.
